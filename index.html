ECHO SHIFT

Every move you make is replayed N turns later by an echo of yourself. Echoes are solid: they block you, they hold down pressure plates, and if one steps into you, that is a paradox. You must move every turn — there is no waiting, so nobody can hold a plate for two turns running and a gate never stays open. It blinks.

Two things bend that. Shards seal the exit until you have collected every one, so the route is never just "reach the door". And a sentinel beside you can be struck — the one turn in the game you spend without moving. That single pause flips your parity, and later makes your echo stand still: the only way a plate is ever held twice running, and the only way a gate stays open instead of blinking.

Legend

  • YOU — the present
  • ECHO — you, N turns ago. Corner ticks mark where it steps next.
  • PLATE — gates open while every plate is held down, which is never longer than one turn
  • GATE
  • SHARD — the exit stays sealed until you carry every one. Echoes walk straight over them; only the present picks things up.
  • SENTINEL — blocks its tile. STRIKE (space) cuts down every sentinel next to you and spends the turn standing still.
  • EXIT — barred while a shard is still out there

The rule that matters: you cannot wait, so a gate blinks for exactly one turn per visit. Two gates in a row means going back to the plate twice, at exactly the right spacing.

The exception: strike while standing on a plate, and the echo replaying that turn stands on it twice running — the gate stays open for two turns instead of one. One swing clears every sentinel around you, so each tile is worth exactly one pause.
